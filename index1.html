Community HealthGuard System Flow & Data Pipeline

10-Step architecture for edge AI assessment, multi-signal validation, and emergency mitigation.

1

Data Acquisition

Wearable smartwatch sensors (MAX30102 Heart Rate/SpO2, MAX30205 Body Temp, MPU6050 Motion) + Community Environmental Sensor nodes.

2

Data Aggregation

Raw sensor telemetry is gathered via BLE and LoRa wireless protocols and structured for edge ingestion.

3

Local Processing & Filtering

On-device filtering executed on Snapdragon Wear Elite microcontroller to remove noise and artifact corruptions.

4

Baseline Establishment

User's resting health profile, chronic disease factors, and historical activity baselines are loaded into local memory.

5

On-Device Edge AI Analysis

Lightweight inference powered by TensorFlow Lite / LiteRT and Gemma 3 1B Small Language Model (SLM) offline.

6

Multi-Signal Risk Assessment

Combines physiological data with environmental threats (Heat, PM2.5, Flood) to prevent false positives from single readings.

7

Personalized Risk Scoring

Assigns a dynamic risk score from 0 to 100 based on individual vulnerability and contextual impact.

8

Risk Categorization

Classifies situation into Low, Medium, High, or Critical threat tier.

9

Alert Generation

Triggers smartwatch haptic vibrations, local sounder, caregiver app push notification, and SMS fallback.

10

Emergency Response & Mitigation

Transmits GPS coordinates, patient bio-summary, and caregiver alert for rapid emergency action.
